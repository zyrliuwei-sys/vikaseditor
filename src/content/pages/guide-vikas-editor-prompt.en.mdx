A useful **Vikas Editor prompt** is an editable brief, not a magic phrase. It tells the model who or what is in the picture, what should remain recognizable and which scene you want to create. The six recipes below are original examples for this guide. Copy one, adapt it to your reference photo and use it in the [image studio](/create/image).

If you are new to the workspace, begin with the [Vikas Editor starting guide](/guides/vikas-editor). You can also browse the [homepage templates](/#templates) to choose a visual direction before writing.

<h2 id="what-is-a-prompt">{'What does a photo-editing prompt do?'}</h2>

A prompt describes the intended change. A reference photo supplies visual information that the words cannot fully capture. “Keep my face recognizable” tells the model what matters, but it does not lock every pixel or guarantee an identical face. A clear source photo and a manageable request are still important.

Distinguish three tasks. **Text-to-image** describes a new scene without a source. **Image editing** asks the model to use an uploaded picture as a reference. **Image-to-video** adds motion to a still image. The recipes here are for image editing. For video, use a separate motion instruction in the [video studio](/create/video).

<h2 id="prompt-structure">{'Build a prompt from five specific parts'}</h2>

1. **Subject:** name the person or object in the reference image. For two people, explicitly say both should remain recognizable.
2. **Keep:** list the few details that matter most, such as face, hairstyle, expression and proportions.
3. **Change:** choose a scene, garment or background. Start with one main change instead of a list of unrelated effects.
4. **Light:** describe direction, softness and colour. “Warm light from the left” is more concrete than “amazing lighting.”
5. **Compose:** say head-and-shoulders, waist-up or full length; mention empty space or unwanted lettering when relevant.

You do not need to include every possible camera term. Contradictory instructions—such as a close-up face, full-body pose and distant subject in the same image—make the brief harder to interpret. Choose the framing before adding polish.

<h2 id="adapt-and-run">{'Copy, adapt and generate'}</h2>

Copy your chosen recipe, open the [image studio](/create/image), upload a JPG, PNG or WebP photo up to 5 MB, and paste the text into the prompt field. Replace details that do not apply. Choose an aspect ratio suitable for the intended placement and check the displayed cost before generating.

Copying a recipe is free. This site includes one free image per account; further images and all videos use purchased credits. Current packages are on the [pricing page](/pricing). The examples do not promise a fixed completion time or an identical result across tools.

<h2 id="questions">{'Prompt questions'}</h2>

### Can I use these prompts in another image tool?

They are plain-language briefs you can adapt to a compatible image-editing tool. Controls, limits and results differ. Check that the tool accepts a reference image if you want the photo used.

### Should I write “8K” or “100% same face”?

Those phrases do not set the actual output resolution or guarantee identity. Choose the available quality controls and describe the concrete details you want preserved.
