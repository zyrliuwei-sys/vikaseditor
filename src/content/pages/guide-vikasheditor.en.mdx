<h2 id="use-it-on-your-phone">{'A simple mobile workflow'}</h2>

1. **Open the homepage in your browser.** Use the navigation menu to choose AI Image or AI Video. Bookmark the exact website after confirming it is the one you want.
2. **Pick the original photo from your device.** If the file is too large or uses an unsupported format, export a JPG, PNG or WebP copy below the 5 MB limit.
3. **Write a short, coherent instruction.** Use the [prompt workshop](/guides/vikas-editor-prompt) for examples. Copy the recipe, then replace clothing or scene details that do not fit.
4. **Review the generation message.** Each account with a verified email can claim one free image here, subject to daily availability. Further images and every video need purchased credits. The [pricing page](/pricing) explains the packages.
5. **Save the finished file promptly.** Keep a local copy rather than relying on a temporary result URL or a browser screenshot.

<h2 id="when-you-get-stuck">{'Match the problem to the next step'}</h2>

**You have a prompt but no image.** A prompt is just text. Open the [image studio](/create/image), add a photo if you want it used as a reference, paste the instructions and submit there.

**The wrong style loaded.** Return to the template page and use its own studio button, or replace the prompt with your chosen recipe. A similar thumbnail does not guarantee identical instructions.

**Your balance looks wrong.** Confirm the domain and signed-in account first. Then check [payment history](/settings/payments). A PayPal approval page or an unpaid order is not evidence that payment completed. Send support the order reference if a completed purchase is missing.

**You wanted a specific creator's resources.** Follow that creator's own published link. This site does not operate their profile, distribute their private files or share their account credentials.
