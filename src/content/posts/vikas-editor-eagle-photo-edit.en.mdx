A **Vikas Editor eagle photo edit** places a person and an eagle in the same imagined scene. The useful starting point is a clear portrait, enough open sky for the wings and a prompt that describes where the bird sits relative to the person. Simply adding “eagle, cinematic, HD” leaves those decisions to the model.

This guide focuses on a finished **still photograph**. Copy an original eagle AI photo editing prompt below, adapt it to your photo and use the [AI photo editor](/create/image). For a moving giant-bird entrance, use the separate [Jatayu AI video prompt tutorial](/blog/jatayu-ai-video-prompt).

These are independent recipes from vikas-editor.net, not another creator's downloadable template or an endorsement by a similarly named channel. The prompts describe intended results; they do not guarantee face matching or a particular composition.

## Choose the composition before choosing the prompt

Decide whether you want a readable portrait or a wide fantasy scene. A close face and an enormous fully visible bird compete for space. In a portrait, put the eagle further back and leave space above the head. In a landscape frame, keep the person large enough to recognize while giving the wings more horizontal room.

Keep the eagle **behind the person**, with a gap between their silhouettes. Wings emerging directly from the shoulders produce a different concept: a winged person. If that is not your intention, say so explicitly. A distant bird can still feel imposing through its placement and lighting without covering the face.

## Prepare one useful reference photo

Choose a sharp picture with the face unobstructed and reasonably even light. A front or three-quarter view is easier to describe than a face hidden by a hand, deep shadow or a strong beauty filter. If the intended result includes a saree or a specific shirt, show enough of that clothing in the source image.

Use a photo you have permission to edit. The studio accepts JPG, PNG and WebP files up to 5 MB. An image can be generated without a reference, but it will not know your appearance from your name or from the words “same face.” A reference supplies information; it still does not guarantee an identical face.

## Make the eagle edit in Vikas Editor

1. Open the [image generator](/create/image) and select **Image**.
2. Under **Choose your style**, select **Your own idea**. Upload the reference photo under **Your photo**.
3. Copy one prompt from this article and paste it into **Describe your idea**, replacing any existing text. Adjust the clothing, setting and light to match your source.
4. Choose **3:4** or **16:9** under **Format**. The format control determines the canvas; typing “8K” into a prompt does not change the output resolution.
5. Check the displayed availability and cost, then generate. Each account can claim one free image subject to daily availability; later images use purchased credits. Sign in when asked.
6. Inspect the face, hands, feather edges and wing tips before downloading. Save the file within the studio's stated download window, currently 24 hours.

The studio's template preview is a style reference. It is not an eagle result generated from your new prompt. Judge the finished output after the job completes. For general account and credit steps, see the [Vikas Editor starting guide](/guides/vikas-editor).

## Questions about the eagle edit

### Is there an Eagle button or a template file to download?

There is no dedicated Eagle template in the current style menu. Use **Your own idea** and paste the prompt. These instructions do not require a CapCut project, VN code or an APK.

### Can I use the prompt without uploading a photo?

Yes, for an invented person or a bird-only scene. Remove the reference-photo instructions and describe the desired subject. Upload a reference when you want the result to resemble a particular consenting person.

### Does “keep my face” guarantee the same face?

No. It expresses a preference to the model. Compare the eyes, nose, jaw, hairstyle and skin tone with the source before sharing the result. Avoid promising someone an exact likeness from an untested prompt.

### Can this still become a Jatayu video?

Yes, it can serve as a starting frame. If the bird is already visible, animate a gentle hover instead of asking it to appear from nowhere. The [Jatayu video tutorial](/blog/jatayu-ai-video-prompt) explains both the reveal approach and the simpler existing-bird approach.
