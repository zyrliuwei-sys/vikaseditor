<h2 id="what-people-mean">{'What people mean by "editing by vikas"'}</h2>

People searching for “editing by vikas” often want a recognisable AI photo-editing look: a cinematic portrait, a richly coloured saree, a festive Diwali scene or a photograph with gentle video motion. The phrase is also associated with creator content. It is not the name of a built-in feature on every website that mentions Vikas, and it does not identify one interchangeable download format.

The look can travel in three different forms. A **CapCut template** is an editing project opened in CapCut, usually with slots for your photos or clips and a prepared sequence of effects. A [VN code or template link](https://www.vlognow.me/help/templates/) points to a project intended for VN; it is not an image prompt that another generator can automatically understand. An **AI prompt** is a written description of the subject, lighting, clothes, setting and motion that you give to a compatible image or video tool.

Before trying a link, check which of those three things you actually need. Vikas Editor AI provides its own prompts and a browser-based creative studio. It does not import CapCut projects or VN codes, and this guide does not distribute another creator’s original files. You can use a visual reference to describe a similar mood while making your own edit.

<h2 id="broken-template-links">{"Why the template links often don't work"}</h2>

A link may point to a project that was removed or made private, or to a share destination that no longer resolves. A repost can also preserve the caption while losing the useful original link. Opening the creator’s current source page is more informative than repeatedly following an old copied address.

Availability can depend on the app, device or region. A template made for a mobile editor may not open as an editable project in a desktop browser, and an unavailable template is not necessarily a problem with your photograph. Check the destination app’s own message before changing your account or paying for access; [CapCut’s template help](https://www.capcut.com/help/use-and-export-templates-in-capcut) explains its app and regional availability.

Names can change as well. A creator may rename a post, a template or an account, while a search result still displays the earlier wording. Compare the publisher and destination rather than assuming every page with a similar name is the same service. If the source project is unavailable, a prompt-based recreation lets you work from the visual idea without depending on that exact link.
