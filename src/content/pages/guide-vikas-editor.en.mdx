An effective **Vikas Editor** photo edit starts with a clear decision: what should change, and what should still look like you? A dramatic background is easy to ask for. Getting the lighting, pose and clothing to work together takes a more deliberate brief. This guide walks through that process using the image studio on [the Vikas Editor homepage](/).

Here, you can browse original visual templates, read their prompts and generate an image from a description or reference photo. The [Vikasheditor navigation guide](/guides/vikasheditor) explains how to choose the right destination when similar names appear in search.

Think of AI editing as a new interpretation of your instructions. A request to preserve a face helps communicate your intention, but it does not guarantee that every feature will remain identical. Plan to inspect the result before using it.

<h2 id="prepare-your-photo">{'Prepare a photo the model can read'}</h2>

Choose one well-lit JPG, PNG or WebP image, no larger than **5 MB**. A sharp original usually gives a clearer reference than a screenshot of a compressed social post. For portraits, keep the eyes visible and avoid a hand, phone or heavy shadow covering the face.

- **Keep useful detail.** A waist-up photograph gives clothing and posture context. A tiny face in a large landscape gives the model less information about the person.
- **Choose a suitable angle.** If the source shows a face straight on, begin with a similar viewpoint. Asking for a completely new profile adds uncertainty.
- **Use an image you may edit.** Get permission before uploading another person's photograph.
- **Keep the original.** If a later variation drifts away from the person, return to that file rather than repeatedly editing an imperfect result.

You can also start without a reference image in image mode. In that case, the person and scene are generated from your description; there is no uploaded identity to preserve.

<h2 id="make-your-first-edit">{'Make your first edit in five steps'}</h2>

1. **Start with a visual direction.** Open the [homepage template gallery](/#templates). Read the full template page before choosing it; the example communicates a mood, not a promised result.
2. **Open the image studio.** A template's use button opens the workspace with its prompt. Alternatively, use [AI Image](/create/image) and write your own brief.
3. **Upload your photo and edit the prompt.** Remove details that do not fit your subject. Keep one location, one lighting idea and one clothing direction. Tell the model which features should remain recognizable.
4. **Choose the frame and review the cost.** Square, portrait and landscape compositions need different amounts of background. Check the free-use or credit message next to generation before you submit.
5. **Generate, inspect and download.** Wait for a final result. Review it at a useful size, then save a local copy while the download is available.

A practical first brief might be: “Use my reference photo for the person. Keep the face, hairstyle and natural expression recognizable. Place the person beside an old city wall at late afternoon, wearing a charcoal jacket. Use warm light from the left, soft background detail and a waist-up composition. No lettering.”

For more directions, the [Vikas Editor prompt workshop](/guides/vikas-editor-prompt) offers six original recipes with copy buttons. Change the scene sentence before adding extra adjectives.

<h2 id="review-the-result">{'Review the photograph, not just the effect'}</h2>

A pleasing thumbnail can hide problems. Look at the eyes, teeth, hairline and visible fingers. Check that earrings, glasses and clothing edges are complete. Examine whether the direction of the face lighting matches the new background. If text appeared unexpectedly, ask for a scene without signs or lettering.

<h2 id="questions">{'Questions before your first edit'}</h2>

### Do I need to install a Vikas Editor app?

This site's studio runs in your browser. Start at the [homepage](/), open a template and continue into the workspace. You do not need a separate APK to use this website.

### Can the tool promise an unchanged face?

No. A clear photo and a short preservation instruction improve the brief, but generated details can differ. Inspect the likeness and repeat from the original when needed.

### Can I turn the image into a video?

The [video studio](/create/video) accepts a source image and a motion prompt. Start with a still image you are happy with, then describe a small camera or subject movement. Video uses purchased credits.
