## About this policy

Vikas Editor AI is an online AI image and video generation website at [vikas-editor.net](https://vikas-editor.net), focused on creators in India and available to users worldwide. This policy explains how information is handled when you browse templates, create an account, generate images or videos, or contact support.

For privacy questions or requests, email [support@vikas-editor.net](mailto:support@vikas-editor.net).

## Photos, prompts and AI generation

Selecting a photo shows a local browser preview. The photo is sent to our server and the AI provider only when you choose Generate. Text-to-image requests send your prompt without requiring a source photo. The application does not save the source photo in its database. It stores your prompt, generation settings, task status, credit use and generated result link under your account so you can revisit your creations.

The current generation integration uses EvoLink to access Nano Banana 2 and Seedance 2.0. Reference photos are uploaded to EvoLink’s temporary file service before generation. Its handling of submitted media is subject to the applicable provider terms and data policies; see [EvoLink’s Privacy Policy](https://evolink.ai/zh/privacy). Our database storage practices do not mean that a provider immediately deletes submitted media. Generated files are hosted by the provider; anyone with a result URL may be able to open it. Save results you want to keep because provider links may expire. Only submit photos you have permission to use, and avoid including sensitive personal information in photos or prompts.

## Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our service:

1. **Account Information** — Your name, email address, and other details you provide when creating an account.
2. **Usage Data** — Information about how you interact with the service, including features accessed, frequency of use, and interaction patterns.
3. **Device Information** — Device type, operating system, and browser type, used to optimize compatibility and performance.
4. **Cookies** — Small data files stored on your device to remember preferences and improve your experience.
5. **Payment Information** — Billing details handled by trusted third-party payment processors. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
6. **Creative Content** — Prompts and photos you submit for generation, generation settings, task records, credit usage and result links.
7. **Support Messages** — Your email address and the information you provide when asking for account, generation, billing or privacy support.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect to:

- Provide account access, generate images and videos, and display your creation history.
- Maintain the service and understand which features are useful to creators.
- Process transactions and send related confirmations.
- Send technical notices, security alerts, and support messages.
- Respond to your questions, feedback, and support requests.
- Detect, prevent, and address fraud and abuse.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only in these limited cases:

- With providers that help deliver AI generation, hosting, account services, payment processing, support or analytics, as needed for those services.
- To comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful requests from public authorities.
- To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users.

## Data Security

We use measures such as account authentication and access controls to protect information. No method of Internet transmission or storage is completely secure. Keep your account credentials and generated result links private, and contact support if you suspect unauthorised access.

## Retention and international processing

Account and task records support your access, creation history, billing and troubleshooting. Some records may need to be retained for legal obligations, disputes or fraud prevention. Contact us to request deletion of information we hold. Removing an account record does not necessarily remove a provider-hosted file or copies you have shared elsewhere.

Although Vikas Editor AI focuses on the Indian market, the service uses third-party infrastructure and AI providers. Information may be processed outside India or your country of residence; using the service does not mean your data stays in India.

## Your choices and privacy requests

You can update the profile fields available in your account settings. To request access, correction, export or deletion of your information, or to raise a privacy concern, email [support@vikas-editor.net](mailto:support@vikas-editor.net) from your account email where possible. We may need to verify account ownership before acting on a request. Applicable rights and any retention requirements depend on the law that applies to you.

You can stop submitting content at any time and contact us about withdrawing consent where processing relies on it. This may affect our ability to provide the related feature. Browser settings let you manage cookies; where the site displays a Cookie Settings control, you can also use it to change available preferences. Disabling essential cookies may prevent sign-in or other account features from working.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, sending a notice through the service.

For privacy questions, data requests or concerns about a photo submitted to Vikas Editor AI, contact [support@vikas-editor.net](mailto:support@vikas-editor.net). Include the relevant task reference if you have one. Do not send passwords or full payment card details.
